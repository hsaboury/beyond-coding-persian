# Contributing to Beyond Coding — Persian Translation

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Persian translation of **Beyond Coding** by David Tuffley.

This is an open-source educational translation project. Feedback, corrections, terminology suggestions, and translation improvements are welcome.

The goal of the contribution process is to improve the quality of the Persian translation while maintaining consistency in terminology, writing style, and editorial decisions throughout the book.

---

## Ways to Contribute

You can contribute to the project by:

- reporting translation errors or unclear passages;
- suggesting better Persian equivalents for technical or professional terms;
- identifying inconsistencies in terminology;
- reporting Persian grammar, punctuation, or typography issues;
- suggesting improvements to readability while preserving the author's meaning;
- submitting Pull Requests with focused corrections or improvements;
- participating in discussions about translation and terminology decisions.

---

## Before Contributing

Before proposing a translation change, please review:

- [`TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md`](TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md) — translation principles, writing style, editorial standards, and review workflow;
- [`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md) — established terminology decisions;
- [`translation/README.md`](translation/README.md) — translation structure and current progress.

Existing terminology decisions should normally be respected unless there is a clear reason to reconsider them.

---

## Reporting an Issue

If you identify a translation problem but do not want to submit a Pull Request, opening a GitHub Issue is a good way to contribute.

When reporting a translation issue, please include where possible:

- the chapter or section;
- the relevant Persian text;
- the nature of the problem;
- your suggested correction or alternative;
- the reasoning behind the suggestion when terminology or interpretation is involved.

Issues may also be used to discuss terminology before making a larger translation change.

---

## Suggesting Terminology Changes

Terminology consistency is an important part of this project.

If you would like to propose a different translation for a term already recorded in [`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md), please explain why the alternative may be:

- more accurate;
- more natural in Persian;
- more widely accepted among Persian-speaking IT professionals;
- more appropriate in the context of the book.

Changing an established glossary term may require reviewing previous occurrences throughout the translation.

For this reason, terminology changes should be considered project-wide decisions rather than isolated word replacements.

---

## Pull Requests

Changes to the `main` branch are made through Pull Requests.

For a contribution:

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for your change.
3. Make a focused set of changes.
4. Review the changes against the project's translation guidelines and glossary.
5. Commit the changes with a clear commit message.
6. Push the branch to your fork.
7. Open a Pull Request against the `main` branch of this repository.
8. Clearly explain what was changed and why.

Whenever possible, keep each Pull Request focused on a single topic or closely related set of changes.

---

## Translation Contributions

Before translating or substantially rewriting a large section or chapter, please open an Issue first to discuss the proposed work.

This helps prevent duplicated effort and ensures that the contribution follows the current translation direction and terminology decisions.

Translation contributions should:

- preserve the meaning and intent of the original author;
- use natural and professional Persian;
- follow established terminology in `GLOSSARY.md`;
- follow the principles defined in `TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md`;
- clearly distinguish translator commentary from the author's original content;
- avoid unnecessary changes unrelated to the purpose of the contribution.

---

## AI-Assisted Contributions

AI-assisted tools may be used for research, terminology exploration, comparison, and review.

However, contributors remain responsible for the content they submit.

Raw AI-generated translations should not be submitted without careful human review.

Contributors should verify:

- accuracy against the original text;
- natural Persian expression;
- terminology consistency;
- preservation of the author's meaning and tone.

AI-generated output is not considered a substitute for human editorial judgment.

---

## Translator Notes

Translator notes should only be added when they provide meaningful value to the reader.

They may be useful for:

- terminology explanations;
- cultural or professional context;
- clarification of translation decisions;
- relevant context for Persian-speaking readers.

Translator notes must always be clearly distinguishable from the original author's content.

Substantial new translator commentary may be discussed in an Issue before being added.

---

## Review and Editorial Decisions

All contributions are subject to editorial review before being merged.

Suggestions and Pull Requests may be:

- accepted;
- revised through discussion;
- incorporated in a different form;
- declined when they conflict with the translation principles or editorial direction of the project.

The project maintainer is responsible for final editorial decisions in order to preserve consistency across the complete translation.

Constructive discussion and disagreement about translation choices are welcome.
