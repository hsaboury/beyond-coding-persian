## Changelog Guidelines

Changes should be recorded when they meaningfully affect the published translation or the way the project is maintained.

Relevant changes may include:

- publication of new translated chapters or sections;
- substantial revisions to existing translations;
- significant terminology decisions or changes;
- changes to translation or editorial guidelines;
- project structure changes;
- licensing or attribution updates.

Minor typo fixes, formatting corrections, and routine maintenance do not normally need individual changelog entries.

---

## Change Categories

When applicable, changes should be grouped under the following categories:

- **Added** — New translations, documentation, or project resources.
- **Changed** — Meaningful changes to existing translations, terminology, or project documentation.
- **Fixed** — Corrections to translation errors or other significant issues.
- **Removed** — Content or project resources intentionally removed.
